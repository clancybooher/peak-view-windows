Repair Services

Hinge Adjustment + Replacement

Door dragging on the floor, swinging open on its own, or not latching right? Almost always a hinge issue. We adjust, tighten, or replace them depending on what's needed.

Door Alignment

Old homes settle over time and doors go out of square. We re-hang, plane, or adjust the frame so the door opens, closes, and seals properly again.

Weatherstripping Replacement

Cold air getting in around the door frame is almost always worn weatherstripping. We replace it around the full door perimeter. Big difference in comfort, quick to do.

Threshold Repair + Replacement

A worn or damaged threshold lets drafts and moisture under the door. We repair or replace it to get a proper seal at the bottom.

Lock + Hardware Replacement

Broken deadbolts, worn handles, stripped strike plates. We source the right hardware and install it. Works on most door brands and styles.

Frame Repair

Rotted, split, or damaged door frames are common in older homes. We repair or replace the affected section so the door hangs and seals correctly.

Door Materials

What We Install

We'll help you pick the right material for your home, your climate, and your budget. All options are available in Energy Star certified configurations.

Fiberglass

Most Popular

The best overall choice for Central Oregon. Doesn't warp, rot, or dent. Excellent insulation. Can be painted or stained to look like real wood. Holds up well in freeze-thaw conditions.

Steel

Best Value

Strong, secure, and affordable. Good insulation values. Can dent if hit hard, but for most homes it's a solid, cost-effective option. Available in a range of styles and finishes.

Wood

Classic Look

Real wood or wood-clad fiberglass. The right choice when you want a traditional look and are willing to maintain it. Requires more upkeep in Central Oregon's climate but looks great.

Common Questions

Door FAQ

My door is sticking. Does it need to be replaced?

Probably not. Sticking doors in older homes are usually a settling or hinge issue. We can often fix it in an hour. We'll come out and take a look before recommending anything.

There's a draft around my door even when it's closed. What's that?

Almost always worn weatherstripping or a damaged threshold. Both are repair jobs, not full replacements. We can usually fix it the same day we come out for the estimate.

What's the most energy-efficient door material?

Fiberglass. It doesn't conduct cold the way steel does, doesn't warp like wood, and holds its seal for a long time. It's what we recommend for most Central Oregon homes.

Can you fix a rotted door frame without replacing the whole door?

Yes, in most cases. We assess how far the rot goes and repair or replace just the affected section of the frame. Whether you need a full door replacement is a separate question.

How long does a door replacement take?

One door is usually a half-day job. Multiple doors can typically be done in a single day. We'll give you an accurate timeframe when we come out.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. We come to your home, look at what you've got, and give you a written quote. No charge and no obligation to book.
