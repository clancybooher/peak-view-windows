Door Materials

Fiberglass, Steel, or Wood — The Honest Trade-Offs

There's no single right answer. The best material depends on your priorities, your budget, and what you're replacing.

Fiberglass

Our most recommended choice for Bend homes. Fiberglass doesn't warp, crack, or rot. It handles the UV exposure and freeze-thaw cycles well, takes paint or stain, and can be textured to look like real wood grain. Higher upfront cost than steel — lower lifetime maintenance cost than either.

  • Doesn't warp or crack in temperature swings
  • Can mimic wood grain appearance
  • Best long-term performance in Central Oregon
  • Paintable or stainable

Steel

Strong, secure, and budget-friendly. Steel doors are a solid choice for homeowners who prioritize security and value. The trade-off: they can dent and are prone to rust if the finish is damaged — not ideal for Bend homes that see significant weather exposure on south or west-facing entries.

  • Most affordable material
  • Strong security performance
  • Good insulation value (polyurethane core)
  • Requires maintenance if finish chips

Wood

The premium aesthetic option. Nothing looks like real wood. Right for custom homes, historic properties, and buyers for whom the look is non-negotiable. Wood doors require regular finishing to prevent warping and swelling — in Central Oregon's dry climate, this is manageable with proper care.

  • Best aesthetic — real wood grain and warmth
  • Right for custom and historic homes
  • Fully paintable and stainable
  • Requires periodic refinishing

Why Installation Matters

A Good Door, Installed Wrong, Is a Bad Door

The most common failure in door replacement isn't the door — it's the install. Doors that aren't plumb and square bind, fail to seal, and wear out their weatherstripping in two years instead of twenty.

We check the rough opening, shim everything level and plumb, and install full weatherstripping before we finish the trim. Then we test the swing and seal before we leave.

This takes more time than a fast swap. It's how we've maintained a 4.9-star rating across 87 reviews and why we still get calls from people we installed for back in 2012.
