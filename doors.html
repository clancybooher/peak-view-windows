Door Replacement  ·  Bend, Oregon

Your Doors Are Letting in the Cold.

Entry doors and patio doors are major energy loss points and one of the most overlooked. A drafty door runs your heating system hard all winter. We install high-performance replacement doors and offer a free evaluation to tell you honestly whether yours needs repair or full replacement.

Why It Matters

Doors Are Where Your Energy Goes

Along with windows, doors are the primary entry point for cold air, heat transfer, and sound infiltration. The fix is often faster and cheaper than homeowners expect.

Energy

Drafty entry and patio doors are among the biggest heat-loss culprits in Central Oregon homes. The right door, properly installed, stops the bleeding fast.

Security

An aging frame, worn weatherstrip, or a door that doesn't hang true is a security gap. We inspect the whole opening. Not just the door slab.

Curb Appeal

Your entry door is the first thing visitors and buyers see. A new door is one of the highest-ROI upgrades you can make to your home's exterior.

Not Sure What You Need?

We'll Tell You. Free.

A drafty door is not automatically a door that needs replacing. Sometimes it's a weatherstripping fix. Sometimes it's a hinge adjustment. Sometimes the frame has rotted and a full replacement is the only right call. We come to your home, evaluate the door and the opening honestly, and give you a clear recommendation. We provide an itemized quote if replacement makes sense. No pressure, no obligation.

Beyond Bend

Door Replacement in Redmond & Sisters

Same free evaluation, same honest repair-or-replace call, same crew. Just a short drive up or down Highway 97 or 20.

Redmond

Redmond's wind exposure is tough on entry doors and older sliding patio doors, especially in neighborhoods built before 2000 where frames have shifted and swollen over the years. We evaluate the door and the opening honestly. Sometimes it's a weatherstripping or hardware fix. Sometimes the frame has gone and a full replacement is the right call.

Sisters

Sisters' higher elevation means colder nights and heavier snow load than Bend, which is hard on door seals and thresholds. Mountain and log-style homes in the area often call for custom sizing and matched trim, handled by the same crew that does the door and the finish carpentry, start to finish.
