Our Story

We didn't stumble into Bend. We chose it — the mountains, the trails, the high desert air, the kind of community where people actually know their neighbors. Clancy and I packed up our lives in Indiana, loaded the dogs, and made the drive west because this is exactly where we wanted to build something worth being proud of.

Clancy has been in the trades for years. Before Peak View he ran a full construction company and a dog boarding operation simultaneously — which, if you know him, makes complete sense. He's the kind of person who is always building something. If it's not a window install, it's his work trailer. If it's not the trailer, it's an AI system he's piecing together at 10pm because he genuinely can't stop solving problems. He is, without exaggeration, the most handy person alive. He personally measures, sources, and leads every single install we do — no exceptions, no subcontractors running jobs on their own.

I'm Naomi. I handle the side of the business that keeps everything running behind the scenes. When I'm not doing that, you'll probably find me near horses — riding, caring for them, or just being around them. It's my reset button the same way the trails are Clancy's.

We spend as much time outside as we possibly can. Hiking, camping, getting into the backcountry whenever the schedule allows. And we do all of it with Thriller, our Golden Retriever, and Bagel, our King Charles Cavalier Spaniel — who takes himself very seriously despite being roughly the size of a large loaf of bread.

We built Peak View because Central Oregon homeowners deserve a window and door company that actually shows up — not a franchise that sends a different crew every time, not a call center that puts you on hold. You call us, you get us. You open the door on install day, you see Clancy. That's the whole idea, and we're not changing it.
