Our Story

Clancy leads every single installation with a steady, perfectionist eye. He brings years of hands-on experience in home remodeling, fine finish carpentry, and expert window and door replacement. He simply loves to create. When he's not on a job site, he's crafting custom wood furniture, throwing pottery, mountain biking, or skiing as much as the weather allows.

Naomi is Clancy's right-hand and the engine behind the business. She handles all the behind-the-scenes work. Phone calls, scheduling, making sure every job runs exactly as it should. Off the clock, she loves cooking good meals, riding and training horses, and exploring Oregon's trails with their dogs.

We started Peak View because Central Oregon homeowners deserve a window and door company that actually shows up. Not a franchise that sends a different crew every time. Not a call center that puts you on hold. You call us, you get us. You open the door on install day, you see Clancy. That's the whole idea, and we're not changing it.
