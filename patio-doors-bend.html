Patio Door Replacement in Bend, Oregon

A tired slider is one of the biggest holes in a Bend house. Large glass, a worn track, and a lock that no longer meets the strike will run the heat all winter.

Door Replacement

Large Glass, Freeze-Thaw, and a Track That Has Given Up

Patio doors fail differently than windows. The glass area is bigger, the moving panel has to stay square on a track through Bend's freeze-thaw, and a 1/8-inch gap at the meeting stile is a noticeable draft. Original 1990s sliders and fogged insulated glass are the usual call — especially on west-facing great rooms.

We replace sliding patio doors, hinged patio doors, and French-style units. Most jobs are Andersen 100 Series. We measure the opening, tell you whether the existing frame can stay, and finish the interior and exterior trim so the new door looks like it was always there.

If you are not sure the door needs replacing, say so. A roller and weatherstrip service is cheaper than a new unit when the frame is still sound. We will tell you that. Free in-home evaluation, CCB #260230.
