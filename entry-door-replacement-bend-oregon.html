Door Replacement

Drafts and a threshold that does not meet

Most drafty-door calls in Bend are not a failed slab. Worn threshold. Weatherstrip cooked by the sun. A hinge that settled. We start there. We replace it when the frame is rotted at the sill, the door is coming apart, or the opening was never flashed.

If it needs a new door, we install one that fits the house. Fiberglass or steel for most Bend winters. Wood if the house needs it. New threshold. New trim. A north-facing entry is a heating bill, not just curb appeal.
