What agentic AI actually is

People hear "agent" and think a robot is about to take the shop. It is simpler than that.

A chatbot waits for you. You type. It answers. Then it sits there.

An agent has a job. Something happens, it does the job, then it stops. That is agentic AI. The word is new. The idea is not. You already have people who watch the inbox and the phone. This is that, on a clock, with a hard stop.

A job, a trigger, a stop

Three parts. Miss one and you have a toy.

  • The job: write a CRM note from a recorded call.
  • The trigger: the call ends in Quo.
  • The stop: draft the follow-up text. Do not send it.

That last part matters more than the first two. If it can spend money, move a job, or talk to a customer for the first time, it waits for you.

Where it lives

The agent can run from Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Hermes, or Claude Cowork. I pick the home that fits how you already work. You keep the account.

The work shows up in the same places you already look. A draft in Gmail. A note in the CRM. A text sitting in Quo. No new dashboard to check at 9 pm.

What it is not

It is not a brain for the company. It does not "know your business." It knows the job you gave it and the files you let it read.

It will be wrong sometimes. That is why the first week is you and me looking at the drafts together. We tighten the job until the misses are rare.

Why shops stall

They open ChatGPT. They ask it a question. They close the tab. Nothing in the CRM moved. Nothing in Quo moved. That is not an agent. That is a search box with manners.

The jump is connecting one tool you already pay for to one model, with one job and one stop. After that is boring, we add the next job.
