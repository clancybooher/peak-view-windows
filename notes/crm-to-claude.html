Wire the CRM first

The job is already in there. The follow-up is what slips.

A lead hits Jobber or HubSpot. You mean to write back. A truck pulls up. Three days later the job is still sitting there with no note.

That is the first wire I like. Not a new app. The CRM you already pay for, talking to Claude or ChatGPT.

What the agent does

A new job lands. The agent reads the request and the last few notes on that customer. It drafts a short email in your voice. Next step is a time, not a pitch. It writes a one-line note back in the CRM so the next person can see what happened.

The email sits as a draft. You send it or you don't.

Why the CRM, not a chat tab

Every chat starts empty. No name. No last note. No quote. You have to paste. You forget. The CRM already has that. Wiring it is how the model stops starting from zero.

The model should read the CRM. It should not become a second list of customers. If I let it invent one, you will have two lists by Friday.

A prompt that holds

Something like this:

"Read this job and the last three notes. Draft a reply in my voice. Short. First name. Offer two times if they asked for a visit. If they are mad, stop and tag me. Save a draft. Write one line back in the CRM. Do not send."

Then we paste two of your real replies so it sounds like you.

How we set it up

Screen share. I connect the CRM to Claude or ChatGPT, whichever home we picked. We run last week's jobs through it. We change one line at a time until the drafts are ones you would send.

You keep the CRM login. You keep the model account. I write down the steps.

If follow-up is the thing that slips, this is a good first wire. Use the form or email clancy@peakvieworegon.com.
