Pick two connectors, not twelve

The shops that stall try to wire everything in week one. Start smaller.

A connector is a wire between a tool you already pay for and a model that can read it. Gmail to Claude. Quo to Claude. Your CRM to ChatGPT. That is it.

You do not need Slack and Teams and Sheets and three CRMs on day one. You need the place work is already dying.

Where I look first

Inbox or phone. Almost always.

If leads come in by form and die in Gmail, we wire Gmail. If the day is calls, we wire Quo. If jobs sit in Jobber and nobody writes a note, we wire the CRM.

One of those three is enough to learn on. The rest can wait.

One model

Pick Claude or ChatGPT or Grok. I will tell you which one fits how you already work. Hermes or Claude Cowork if that is the better home for the agent. We do not run the same job in four places. That is how you lose the thread.

The second wire

After the first one is boring, add the next. CRM to Claude is a common second. The agent already drafts from email. Now it can see the job history too. The reply gets better because it knows what you already quoted.

Or Quo to Claude, if the inbox is fine and the calls are the mess. The recording becomes a note. The note lands in the CRM. You still send the text.

What I skip

I skip tools you opened once and forgot. I skip anything with no API and no export unless the job is worth a custom connector. I skip a suite that wants you to move your whole shop into it.

If a wire would only save you two minutes a week, we do not build it. Time is the test.
