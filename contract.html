1. Scope of Work

Peak View Windows and Doors ("Contractor") agrees to furnish all labor, materials, and equipment necessary to complete the window and/or door replacement work described in your individual quote. The specific scope — including product selections, quantities, and installation locations — is set out in your signed quote.

All work will be performed in a workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards. Work not listed in your quote is excluded and subject to a separate written Change Order.

2. Materials

Contractor will supply and install the materials specified in your quote. Substitutions of equivalent or better quality may be made if specified materials become unavailable. You will be notified of any substitution before installation.

Window and door units are custom-ordered for your specific openings and are generally non-returnable once ordered.

3. Contract Price & Payment Terms

Your total contract price is shown on your individual quote. No Oregon sales tax applies.

Deposit Rule

If materials exceed labor cost: The deposit equals the full cost of materials — so Contractor can place your custom order. Materials are non-refundable once ordered.

If labor equals or exceeds material cost: The deposit is 50% of the total contract price, due before work begins.

The exact deposit amount and balance due are shown on your quote. The balance is due upon completion of work and final walkthrough.

Payment is accepted by check (payable to Peak View Windows and Doors), cash, or bank transfer. A $35 fee applies to returned checks.

4. Schedule & Access

Estimated start date and duration are listed on your quote. You agree to provide reasonable access to the property on scheduled work days. If you are not present, Contractor may complete the work provided safe access is available.

Delays caused by weather, material lead times, or factors outside Contractor's control extend the schedule and do not constitute a breach of this contract.

5. Change Orders

Any work outside the scope in your original quote — including but not limited to:

  • Structural damage, rot, or mold discovered during removal of existing units
  • Non-standard rough openings requiring framing modification
  • Additional windows, doors, or openings requested by Client after signing
  • Material upgrades requested after materials are ordered
  • Hazardous materials (lead paint, asbestos) requiring special handling

…requires a written Change Order signed by both parties before additional work begins. The Change Order will specify the additional scope, materials, cost, and any schedule impact.

Contractor will notify you immediately upon discovering any condition that warrants a change order. No additional charges will ever be made without your written approval.

6. Warranties

2 Years

Installation Warranty

Contractor warrants all installation workmanship for 2 years from the date of completion. Covers air infiltration, water infiltration, and operational defects (racking, binding, improper sealing) directly attributable to Contractor's installation work.

Varies

Manufacturer Warranty

All window and door units carry the manufacturer's own product warranty. Coverage, duration, and transferability vary by brand and product line and are set by the manufacturer, not Contractor. Contractor will assist you in submitting warranty claims.

Warranties are void if: (a) work is altered or repaired by parties other than Contractor without prior written consent; (b) damage results from settling, foundation movement, structural shifts, or acts of nature; (c) Client fails to maintain weatherstripping, caulking, hardware, or finish per manufacturer guidelines; (d) units are subjected to abnormal use, impact, or water intrusion from sources unrelated to the installation.

7. Insurance & Liability

Contractor carries general liability insurance and workers' compensation as required by Oregon law (CCB #260230). Contractor is not liable for:

  • Pre-existing conditions at the property
  • Cosmetic damage to interior finishes (paint, drywall) incidental to window/door removal — Contractor will minimize disturbance but some touch-up may be required
  • Damage resulting from Client-supplied materials or Client-directed work
  • Consequential damages beyond the value of the contract

Contractor's total liability under any contract shall not exceed the contract price.

8. Permits

Like-for-like window and door replacement generally does not require a building permit under Bend/Deschutes County guidelines. When a permit is required (new openings, structural work, or egress windows), Contractor will obtain all required permits and permit fees will be included in or added to the contract price. The specific permit situation for your job will be addressed in your individual quote.

9. Cleanup & Disposal

Contractor will remove all debris, packaging, and old units from the property upon project completion. Hazardous material disposal (if encountered) will be handled per Oregon DEQ requirements and billed as a change order if not anticipated in the original scope.

10. Cancellation

  • You may cancel within 3 business days of signing or accepting your quote with no penalty (Oregon consumer protection law).
  • After 3 business days: if materials have been ordered, you are responsible for actual material costs incurred.
  • If you cancel after materials are delivered or work has begun, you owe the full deposit plus any additional costs incurred.
  • Contractor may cancel if site conditions are unsafe or permit approval is denied; in that case, any deposit is refunded minus materials already ordered.

11. Dispute Resolution

If an issue comes up, we'll talk it out first. If we can't resolve it directly, disputes are handled through binding arbitration under Oregon law in Deschutes County. The prevailing party is entitled to reasonable attorney fees.

12. Entire Agreement

These terms, together with your individual signed quote, constitute the entire agreement between the parties. No verbal promises or prior communications are binding unless incorporated in writing. Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.
