      /Why custom

      Built for you beats renting a suite

      Always-on ops help is a good idea. The big all-in-one suites prove people want it. The difference here is fit and cost.

      I write the automation around how your business already runs. It uses your Gmail, your calendar, your sheet. There's no per-seat fee that climbs every year, and no template you have to bend your day around.

      Most small shops need five or six well-built routines, not a platform. Build them once. Run them for the cost of hosting and API usage. Change them when the business changes.
