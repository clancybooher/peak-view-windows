/What changes

Each one of those has a fix.

Here's what the bot takes off your desk, one problem at a time. The packages below are how you get there.

Drowning in the inbox

You open Gmail to drafts, not a pile.

The chief of staff bot reads every message before you do. It sorts, labels, and drafts the replies you would have written anyway, in your words. Ten minutes at 7 am. Nothing sends without you.

Follow-ups that die

Every quote gets a clock.

Leads, quotes, supplier requests. The bot times each one, nudges when the clock runs out, and tells you the minute someone answers. Nothing goes quiet because you forgot.

CRM and QuickBooks don't talk

A closed job becomes an invoice.

I write the connector. Customer, line items, deposit, all carried over. A paid invoice marks the job paid in the CRM. Nobody retypes anything.

Too many tabs open

One text at 9 pm with what matters.

Tomorrow's schedule, the money that's late, the three emails that need you. One message. Your crew asks the bot in Slack or Teams instead of texting you at 6 am.

Can't afford another admin yet

The desk work costs a fixed build.

A bot handles the repeat work for a set price and a small monthly. Hire people for the work that needs people.

      /Connectors

      Your software finally talks to itself.

      CRM, QuickBooks, Gmail, Slack or Teams, calendar. You already pay for these. I write the connectors so a change in one shows up in the others, with or without a bot on top.

      • CRM QuickBooks

        A closed job becomes an invoice. A payment marks the job paid. Same customer record on both sides.

      • Gmail CRM

        A new customer email creates the contact and logs the thread. No copy and paste.

      • Form Calendar Text

        A website lead gets a text back with two open slots, a booked hold, and a reminder the day before.

      • Calendar Slack or Teams

        Tomorrow's schedule posts to the crew channel every evening. Changes post as they happen.

      • QuickBooks Text

        A past due invoice gets one polite nudge in your words. Still unpaid a week later and it hands the thread to you.
