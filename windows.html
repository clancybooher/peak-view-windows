Frame Materials

Vinyl, Fiberglass, or Wood-Clad — What's Right for Your Home?

Each material has real trade-offs. We'll give you a straight answer for your situation — not whatever has the highest margin.

Vinyl

The most budget-friendly option with solid performance. Quality vinyl windows hold up well in Central Oregon's climate. The caveat: cheaper vinyl expands and contracts in temperature swings — we only install brands that have proven themselves here.

  • Most affordable frame material
  • Good insulation value
  • Low maintenance
  • Good for most Bend homes

Fiberglass

The premium choice for Central Oregon. Fiberglass doesn't expand and contract like vinyl, so it holds a tighter seal over time. Higher upfront cost — worth it for homes where long-term performance matters more than first cost.

  • Superior dimensional stability
  • Best long-term seal performance
  • Handles UV and temperature extremes
  • 20–30% more than vinyl — often worth it

Wood-Clad

Wood interior with a protective aluminum or fiberglass exterior shell. The warmest aesthetic option — if you're restoring a historic Bend home or want natural wood sills and casings inside, this is the right choice. Requires more care than vinyl.

  • Warmest interior aesthetic
  • Right for historic or custom homes
  • Paintable interior wood
  • Protected exterior — lower maintenance than solid wood
