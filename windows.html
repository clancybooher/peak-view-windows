Window Replacement  ·  Bend, Oregon

Replace Your Windows. Lower Your Bills. Quiet Your Rooms.

Windows are one of the biggest sources of energy loss in most homes. The Department of Energy puts heat gain and loss through windows at 25 to 30% of a home's heating and cooling energy use. We replace them with premium, climate-rated windows built for Central Oregon's high desert, measured, installed, and finished by a licensed local crew that does this every day.

Any Window, Any Home

Replacing drafty single-pane windows is what we do most, and what we do best. Swapping tired aluminum frames for something that actually holds heat? We do that too. And if you have a wall you want to open up and flood with light, we handle new openings start to finish as well.

Why It Matters

The Biggest Efficiency Factor in Your Home

Before air sealing, insulation upgrades, or new HVAC, windows and doors are where heat and cold move the most. Replacing them is the single highest-impact efficiency upgrade most homeowners can make.

Energy Savings

The Department of Energy attributes 25 to 30% of a home's heating and cooling energy use to its windows. ENERGY STAR estimates replacing single-pane windows with certified units cuts household energy bills by up to 13%.

Year-Round Comfort

Old single-pane windows radiate cold in winter and heat in summer. Properly insulated windows eliminate drafts and keep every room comfortable regardless of season.

Noise Reduction

Quality double-pane glass dramatically reduces exterior noise. Road traffic, wind, and neighborhood sounds stay outside where they belong.

The Windows We Recommend

Andersen 100 & 400 Series

For most Central Oregon homes we recommend the Andersen 100 Series. Its Fibrex® composite frame is twice as strong as vinyl and blocks thermal transfer far better than aluminum, so it handles Bend's swing from below zero to the low nineties without warping, rotting, or fading. The black-on-black finish is the most requested look we quote.

When a project calls for a real wood interior you can stain or paint to match existing millwork, we step up to the 400 Series.
