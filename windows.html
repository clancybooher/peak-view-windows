Why It Matters

The Biggest Efficiency Factor in Your Home

Before air sealing, insulation upgrades, or new HVAC, windows and doors are where heat and cold move the most. Replacing them is the single highest-impact efficiency upgrade most homeowners can make.

Energy Savings

Up to 30% of your heating and cooling escapes through inefficient windows. Modern dual-pane low-E glass seals that leak and lowers your utility bill year-round.

Year-Round Comfort

Old single-pane windows radiate cold in winter and heat in summer. Properly insulated windows eliminate drafts and keep every room comfortable regardless of season.

Noise Reduction

Quality double-pane glass dramatically reduces exterior noise. Road traffic, wind, and neighborhood sounds stay outside where they belong.

Frame Materials

Vinyl, Fiberglass, or Wood-Clad

We'll give you a straight recommendation for your home and budget.

Vinyl

Most affordable. Solid insulation performance. The right call for most Central Oregon homes when you use a quality brand built for temperature swings.

Fiberglass

Our top recommendation for this climate. Holds a tighter seal than vinyl over time. Doesn't expand and contract with Bend's temperature swings. Best long-term performance.

Wood-Clad

Interior warmth of real wood with a protected exterior shell. The premium aesthetic choice for custom and historic homes. Paintable inside and out.
