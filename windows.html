Not every window problem needs a full replacement. We'll tell you straight what it actually needs.

Repair Services

Seal Repair

Foggy or cloudy glass between the panes means the seal has failed. We fix the seal or replace just the glass unit so you don't have to replace the whole window.

Glass-Only Replacement

Cracked or broken pane but the frame is fine? We replace just the glass. Faster and cheaper than a full window replacement.

Screen Repair + Replacement

Torn, bent, or missing screens. We repair on-site or replace with new mesh. Quick turnaround.

Weatherstripping

Drafts around the window frame are almost always a weatherstripping issue. We replace it and reseal — big impact, low cost.

Hardware + Locks

Broken latches, handles, cranks, or locks. We source the right parts and install them. Works on most window brands.

Replacement Services

Full Window Replacement

Old window out, new Energy Star window in. We handle everything including disposal of the old unit. Most installs done in one day.

Insert Replacement

If the frame is solid but the window unit is shot, we can do an insert replacement — new window fitted into the existing frame opening. Lower cost, less disruption.

Non-Standard + Custom Sizing

Older homes rarely have standard window sizes. We measure precisely and source windows that fit your actual opening — not the other way around.

Multi-Window Projects

Replacing windows throughout the whole house? We schedule efficiently so the job gets done without dragging on. Most full-house projects wrap in 1 to 2 days.

Window Types We Work On

Whatever

Double-Hung

The most common window style. Both sashes slide up and down. Very common in pre-2000 Oregon homes.

Casement

Hinged on one side, opens outward with a crank. Great seal when closed — good choice for energy efficiency.

Sliding

One panel slides horizontally past the other. Simple, reliable, and common on older Central Oregon ranches.

Picture / Fixed

Non-opening window. Great for views, very airtight. Common as a center panel in combination windows.

Awning

Hinged at the top, swings outward from the bottom. Can stay open even in rain. Common above sinks and in bathrooms.

Bay + Bow

Angled or curved windows that project out from the wall. Common in older character homes. We measure and fit these carefully.

Common Questions

Window FAQ

My window is foggy between the panes. Does it need full replacement?

Not necessarily. Foggy glass usually means the sealed unit has failed and moisture got in between the panes. In many cases we can replace just the glass unit without touching the frame. Give us a call and we'll come take a look.

Can you just replace a broken pane without replacing the whole window?

Yes, if the frame is in good shape. We can replace single panes or sealed double-pane units. It's faster and usually much cheaper than a full replacement.

How do I know if I need repair or replacement?

That's what the free estimate is for. We come out and look at the frame condition, the glass, the seals, and the hardware. If repair makes more sense, we'll tell you. We're not going to push a full replacement when a fix will do the job.

My house was built in the 1970s and the windows are all weird sizes. Can you still help?

Yes. Older homes with non-standard or custom window sizes are most of what we work on. We measure your actual openings and source windows to fit.

Are your windows Energy Star certified?

Yes. All replacement windows we install are Energy Star certified for the Pacific Northwest climate zone. That matters a lot in Central Oregon winters.

How long does installation take?

Most jobs are done in a day. A full house with a lot of windows might run into a second day. We'll give you a realistic timeframe when we come out for the estimate.
