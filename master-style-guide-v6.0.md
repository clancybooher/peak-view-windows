## ZERO-SLOP RULES (MANDATORY — NO EXCEPTIONS)

1. **No em-dashes** unless explicitly requested.
2. **No parallelism or triads** — do not write "We do X, Y, and Z" lists disguised as copy.
3. **No "It's not X, it's Y" constructions.**
4. **No generic analogies** not rooted in Central Oregon reality.
5. **No filler** — every sentence must earn its place.
6. **No unearned profundity** — do not try to sound deep.
7. **Start paragraphs with concrete local sensory details** — cold air, high desert wind, mountain views, specific neighborhoods.
8. **Wild rhythm**: short sentences, one-sentence paragraphs, fragments, natural "Look." at the start of a thought.
9. **Active voice, natural contractions, controlled imperfection** — write how Clancy would actually talk.
10. **Extreme Central Oregon specificity only** — no fabrication, no made-up details.
