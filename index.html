Our Process

Our Simple, Low-Stress Process

We come to you, measure every opening precisely, and give you a firm quote before anything gets ordered. On install day, Clancy leads every opening. When we leave, your home is clean.

01

Free Evaluation

We come to your home, look at your current windows or doors, talk through clear options, and take precise measurements. No showroom visit. No pressure.

02

Firm, Fair Quote

You receive a clear, completely itemized line-by-line quote with zero hidden fees. The price we quote is the exact price you pay.

03

Professional Installation

Clancy personally leads the install from start to finish, protects your living space, and ensures an airtight, weatherproof fit.
