Our Services

If your home was built before 2000, your windows and doors are probably working harder than they should be. We replace them with Energy Star products built for Central Oregon winters.

Window Replacement

Old windows let cold air in and run your heating bill up. We pull them out and put in properly fitted, energy-efficient replacements that actually seal. Older homes with non-standard sizing are no problem.

Door Replacement

New Installations

Common Questions

FAQ

Things people usually ask before they call.

Do you work on older homes built before 2000?

Yes, and it's most of what we do. Older homes in Central Oregon tend to have non-standard window sizes, original wood frames, and other quirks that make a straightforward swap harder. We're used to it.

What cities do you serve?

Bend, Redmond, Sisters, Tumalo, Sunriver, La Pine, and the surrounding area. If you're somewhere in between, just give us a call and we'll figure it out.

How long does the job take?

Most replacements are done in a day. Larger jobs with a lot of windows can run into a second day. We'll give you a realistic timeframe when we come out for the estimate.

Are your windows Energy Star certified?

Yes. We install Energy Star certified windows rated for the Pacific Northwest climate zone. If you're in an older home with single-pane glass, the difference in how warm your house stays is pretty noticeable.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, take a look, and give you a written quote. No charge, no pressure to book.
