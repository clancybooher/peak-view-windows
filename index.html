The Windows We Recommend

Andersen 100 Series

We recommend the Andersen 100 Series for most Central Oregon homes. Its Fibrex® composite frame is twice as strong as vinyl and blocks thermal transfer far better than aluminum, so it holds up to Bend's swing from below zero to the low 90s without warping, rotting, or fading. When a project calls for real wood interiors, we step up to the 400 Series.
