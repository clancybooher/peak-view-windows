Window Replacement in Prineville, Oregon

Prineville is Crook County, not a Bend suburb. Ranch and in-town stock, wider temperature swings, and a lot of mid-century aluminum still doing work it was never rated for.

Serving Prineville

Crook County Climate and Ranch-Town Housing

Prineville sits east of the Cascades rain shadow with a more continental swing than Bend's pine neighborhoods. Summer heat on west walls and hard winter nights both punish aluminum frames and tired dual-pane seals. Ochoco-facing lots and older in-town bungalows show it first: drafts, ice on the interior glass, and sashes that no longer lock square.

Housing here is a mix of ranch and farm houses, mid-century in-town homes, and newer commuter builds along the Highway 126 corridor. The older openings often need full-frame replacement and honest millwork. The newer ones are more often a failed IGU in an otherwise sound frame. We tell you which is which after we measure — not from a photo.

We run Prineville as part of the same Central Oregon service area as Redmond and Bend. Andersen 100 Series for most jobs, 400 Series when you want a wood interior, free in-home quote, CCB #260230.
