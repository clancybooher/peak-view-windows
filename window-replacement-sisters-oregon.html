Window Replacement in Sisters, Oregon

Sisters sits higher in the Cascades foothills than Bend, with colder overnight lows, heavier snow, and a lot of log and mountain-style walls that were never a standard rough opening.

Serving Sisters

Elevation, Snow, and Custom Openings

Sisters is not a slightly colder Bend. Overnight lows run harder, snow load is heavier, and many homes sit in pine cover that keeps north walls cold and damp through long winters. A tired single-pane or early dual-pane unit works overtime here in a way a similar window in a sunnier Bend subdivision does not.

Log homes, cabins, and mountain-style houses around Sisters and the Highway 20 corridor often need custom sizing and trim that matches existing millwork. That is finish carpentry, not a drop-in vinyl insert. We measure the opening, match the surround, and install with the same crew that does the carpentry — not a separate trim sub.

We recommend the Andersen 100 Series for most Sisters replacements: a Fibrex composite frame that holds up to UV, freeze-thaw, and dark exterior colors without the warping vinyl is known for in high-elevation sun. When the interior needs real wood to meet existing stain or paint, we step up to the 400 Series. CCB #260230.
